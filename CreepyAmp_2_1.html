    ◈ CREEPY AMP — USER GUIDE

    ▸ LOAD MUSIC

    • Use the ⏏ button, click the CD slot, or drag & drop files anywhere. MP3/OGG/FLAC/WAV + MP4/WebM.

    ▸ STAGE MODE (double-click on the screen)

    • Double-click the visualizer or a video: the screen alone goes fullscreen — interface and playlist disappear. Exit with Esc or double-click.
    • Video: a controller appears when the mouse moves (title, play/pause, seek bar, track buttons, PiP) and fades after 2.5 s idle. The cursor hides with it.

    ▸ SPATIAL SOUND (live, no restart needed)

    • SPATIAL menu: Original Sound (bypass), Expander 3D (Mid/Side widening — needs stereo content) or QSound System (crossed delays — "around the head", best with headphones).
    • The 3D knob sets the intensity of the selected mode, applied instantly while playing.
    • A safety limiter protects your ears and speakers from peaks when widening is extreme.
    • Tip: mono files are unaffected by the Expander — use QSound, or stereo sources.

    ▸ PLAYBACK

    • Seek bar above the equalizer · X: 2.5 s crossfade · equalizer presets (EQ menu).
    • Volume always starts at maximum; M mutes/restores during the session. The VU meter shows the program level before the volume knob (studio convention).
    • Pressing PLAY at the very end of the last track restarts it.

    ▸ MILKDROP EFFECTS (BUTTERCHURN 2.6.7)

    • Automatic on the big screen for AUDIO; disabled for VIDEO. Arrows ◀▶, click the screen, V/RND (random).
    • AUTO: auto rotation — interval set by the FX TIME knob (5 to 60 s).

    ▸ PLAYLIST & MISC

    • LIST/L: sliding bay · filter box · drag titles to reorder. Drop on the empty area below the list to move a track to the end.
    • PiP video picture-in-picture · ⛶/F fullscreen of the whole app / cinema.
    • Palette button / T: VFD theme — Amber, LCD Green, Ice Blue, Pink, Yellow (this guide follows the theme).
    • EQ, spatial mode, 3D intensity, FX interval, modes and theme are remembered between sessions.

    ▸ SHORTCUTS

    • ␣ Play/Pause · ←→ seek ±5s · ↑↓ volume · M mute
    • N/P track · R repeat · S shuffle · X crossfade
    • L playlist · V visual · T theme · F fullscreen · H help
